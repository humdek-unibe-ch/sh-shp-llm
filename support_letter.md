# Letter of Support for a Permanent University LLM Service

**Technologieplattform Uni Bern (TPF)**  
University of Bern  

23 March 2026

To whom it may concern,

On behalf of the Technologieplattform Uni Bern (TPF), we would like to express our strong support for establishing the University of Bern LLM service based on GPUStack as a permanent centrally operated service.

For our team, this service is not only an interesting proof of concept, but a strategically important building block for productive university systems. We are already integrating the university LLM infrastructure into our SelfHelp CMS through the `sh-shp-llm` plugin and the `sh-shp-llm_therapy_chat` extension. SelfHelp is already used in more than 40 instances at the University of Bern, which means this infrastructure has immediate relevance far beyond a single pilot project.

The availability of a centrally provided university LLM service is especially important because our use cases are directly embedded in operational platforms used in university contexts. Current and near-term use cases include:

- LLM-supported features inside the SelfHelp CMS through our shared `sh-shp-llm` integration.
- Therapy chat workflows through `sh-shp-llm_therapy_chat`, including AI-assisted patient communication, therapist support, draft generation, summaries, and safety-related escalation workflows.
- A planned new SelfHelp CMS version in which administrators can describe the page or content they need in a chat interface, and the LLM will generate a structured result that can be inserted directly into the CMS.

These are not isolated experiments. They are practical product integrations that support real editorial, therapeutic, and administrative workflows. For these scenarios, a local university-operated LLM service is highly valuable because it provides a basis for data protection, institutional control, consistent access, and long-term maintainability. It also avoids fragmented project-by-project solutions and lowers the barrier for additional university services to adopt AI responsibly.

From our perspective, the move from PoC to production is therefore both necessary and timely. A permanent service would give projects like ours the confidence to build on this infrastructure in a sustainable way. In particular, reliability, clear governance, security and compliance processes, and sufficient capacity are essential if the service is to support production use in central university applications.

We therefore explicitly support:

- the continuation of the GPUStack service as a permanent university offering,
- the investment needed to provide resilient and scalable infrastructure,
- the establishment of a clear operating and governance model for model lifecycle and service ownership,
- and the compliance and security measures required for broader productive use.

In summary, the university LLM service already enables meaningful innovation in systems that are used across the University of Bern, and its importance will increase further with the next generation of CMS-supported AI workflows. For TPF, this service is a key enabler for delivering useful, integrated, and institutionally controlled AI functionality to a broad university user base.

We would welcome the continuation and formalization of this service and strongly recommend supporting the transition from proof of concept to production.
